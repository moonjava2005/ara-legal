Ara: your child’s health book

Ara keeps your child’s illnesses, fevers and medicines, doctor visits, vaccines and growth in one calm place on your phone.

Help and questions

Write to moonjava@gmail.com. Tell us your phone model and what you were doing, and we will answer as soon as we can.

Your data

Everything you enter is kept on your phone. If you turn on backup in Settings, a copy also goes to your own Google Drive, and nowhere else. Ara has no account and no server of its own, so nobody at Ara can see your data. You can turn backup off or delete the copy from Settings.

Medical advice

Ara records what happened and what your doctor said. It never suggests doses, the time of the next dose or a diagnosis. Always follow your doctor’s advice.
