Ara privacy policy

Last updated: 11 November 2026

Ara is a health book for your child that you keep on your own phone. This policy explains what happens to the information you enter.

What Ara stores

Everything you enter in Ara stays on your phone: your child's name, birth date and photo, illnesses, temperatures, medicine doses, doctor visits, prescription and vaccine booklet photos, vaccinations and growth measurements. Photos you take or pick are copied into the app's own storage on the phone.

What Ara sends

Ara has no account and does not send your child's information anywhere. There are no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

The only network requests Ara makes are to check for and download app updates (the update service learns the app's version and platform, nothing you entered).

Camera and photos

Ara asks for the camera and the photo library only when you choose to take or pick a photo, and only to attach that photo to a record.

Deleting your data

Deleting a record in Ara deletes it and its photos from the phone. Uninstalling Ara deletes all of its data. Because the data is only on your phone, nobody else, including us, can recover it.

Medical information

Ara records what happened and what your doctor said. It does not give medical advice, suggest doses or make diagnoses.

Children

Ara is used by parents to keep records about their children. It does not collect information from children.
