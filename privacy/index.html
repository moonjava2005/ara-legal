Ara privacy policy

Last updated: 14 November 2026

Ara is a health book for your child that you keep on your own phone. This policy explains what happens to the information you enter.

What Ara stores

Everything you enter in Ara is stored on your phone: your child's name, birth date and photo, illnesses, temperatures, medicine doses, doctor visits, prescription and vaccine booklet photos, vaccinations and growth measurements. Photos you take or pick are copied into the app's own storage on the phone.

Backup to your Google Drive (optional)

Backup is off until you turn it on in Settings. When you turn it on, you sign in with your own Google account and Ara keeps a copy of all your records and photos in its own folder in your Google Drive. The folder is private to Ara: it does not appear among your Drive files, and Ara can only see this folder, not your other files. The copy counts in your Google storage.

The copy goes only to your Google account. Ara has no account of its own and runs no server that receives what you enter, so nobody at Ara can see the copy. Google's own privacy policy applies to what Google stores for you.

You can turn backup off in Settings (the copy stays in your Drive) or delete the copy from your Drive from inside Ara. Removing Ara's access in your Google account settings also deletes the copy.

What Ara sends

Ara sends your child's information nowhere except the backup in your own Google Drive, and only if you turn it on. There are no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

The other network requests Ara makes are to check for and download app updates (the update service learns the app's version and platform, nothing you entered).

Camera and photos

Ara asks for the camera and the photo library only when you choose to take or pick a photo, and only to attach that photo to a record.

Deleting your data

Deleting a record in Ara deletes it and its photos from the phone; the backup, if it is on, follows at its next run. Uninstalling Ara deletes all of its data on the phone. To delete the backup, use "Delete backup from Google Drive" in Settings, or remove Ara's access in your Google account. We hold no copy, so without the backup nobody, including us, can recover deleted data.

Medical information

Ara records what happened and what your doctor said. It does not give medical advice, suggest doses or make diagnoses.

Children

Ara is used by parents to keep records about their children. It does not collect information from children.
